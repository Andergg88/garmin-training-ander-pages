# Garmin Training Ander

Garmin Training Ander is a private personal project for automatically collecting, storing and analysing the owner's Garmin training and health data.

The application is intended exclusively for personal use.

It connects Garmin training and health information with the owner's private Google Drive storage for longitudinal training analysis.

## Purpose

- Store Garmin activity files.
- Store training metrics.
- Store sleep and recovery information.
- Maintain a personal training history.
- Support longitudinal running and trail-running analysis.

## Privacy

Personal data is not sold, shared or distributed to third parties.
