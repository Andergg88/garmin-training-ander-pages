# Privacy Policy – Garmin Training Ander

Garmin Training Ander is a private application created for personal use.

The application processes only data belonging to its owner for the purpose of storing and analysing personal Garmin training and health information.

Google Drive access is used solely to store and manage files generated by this personal application.

The application does not sell, share, distribute, or disclose personal data to third parties.

The application is not offered as a public service and has no external users.

Access to Google Drive can be revoked at any time from the user's Google Account security settings.
